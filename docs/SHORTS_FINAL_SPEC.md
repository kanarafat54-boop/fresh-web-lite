# Fresh Shorts — Final Product Specification

## Core experience
- Full-screen immersive viewer.
- Up/down navigation for the media stream.
- Left/right navigation for creator, context, related media, evidence and source spaces.
- Keyboard, buttons and accessibility alternatives for every gesture.
- Intent modes: Discover, Learn, Research, News, Relax and Create.
- Quiet mode and anti-infinite-scroll controls.

## Supported media
Shorts can contain or reference video, images, galleries, audio, text, live broadcasts and long-form video.

## Comments and replies
Every Short can support a unified comment composer with:
- text comments;
- video comments;
- audio/voice comments;
- image comments;
- galleries/multiple images;
- mixed attachments;
- replies and threaded replies;
- mentions and links;
- edit/delete/report/moderation controls;
- creator pinning and reply highlighting.

Comment attachments use the same media pipeline as first-class Fresh media. They are not treated as opaque blobs.

## Reactions
Support standard reactions plus richer semantic reactions:
- Like, Love, Laugh, Wow, Celebrate, Support;
- Curious, Inspire, Insightful, Agree, Disagree, Helpful, Question, Respect;
- Fire, Sad, Angry;
- future custom reactions defined by Fresh without changing the core interaction model.

Reaction analytics must not force all reactions into a single engagement score. The system should preserve reaction intent.

## Creator Studio
- Record or upload.
- Multi-clip editing, trim, split, reorder, crop and speed.
- Text, captions, stickers, effects and filters.
- Voice-over, music and sound mixing.
- Cover selection.
- Drafts/autosave.
- Publish, schedule and visibility controls.
- AI captions, translation, reframing, highlight extraction and long-video-to-Short generation.

## Media relationships
- Long video -> Shorts.
- Short -> original/full video.
- News -> Short -> full story.
- Live -> highlight -> Short -> replay.
- Post -> related media -> context.
- Remix lineage preserves attribution from original through every remix.

## Trust and safety
- Provenance and source information.
- AI-assisted/generated disclosure where applicable.
- Copyright and reporting controls.
- Block, mute and not-interested controls.
- Clear distinction between confirmed, supported, disputed and unknown information.

## Interaction principle
Shorts is not a one-dimensional infinite-scroll product. It is a navigable media space where movement, context, creation, conversation and knowledge are connected.
